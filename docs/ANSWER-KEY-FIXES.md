# Answer keys that were corrected

Each entry below was flagged by a study chapter, then independently
re-examined before anything was changed. Only flags confirmed outright
were applied; teaching nuances and defective questions were left alone.

The corpus is not authoritative — 41% of its explanations are machine-written
and every paper from 2025 on is a memory-based student recall — which is why
these were worth checking. It follows that this list is not authoritative
either. **Verify anything that matters against your textbook.**

## Answer keys changed

### `neetpg-2013-s1-q0647` — Forensic Medicine (AIPGMEE 2013)

*Quantum of punishment in dowry death is ?*

- **Was:** D. 10 years
- **Now:** A. 7years

Section 304B IPC (now Section 80 BNS) reads 'shall not be less than seven years but which may extend to imprisonment for life', so the statutory minimum is seven, not ten; Reddy and Modi both give seven years, and the stored explanation's own text misquotes the section.

### `neetpg-2013-s1-q1276` — Medicine (AIPGMEE 2013)

*Which of the following is not feature of distal renal tubular acidosis*

- **Was:** A. Normal anion gap
- **Now:** D. Hyperkalemia

A normal (hyperchloraemic) anion gap is a defining feature of every RTA including type 1, so option A cannot be the exception, whereas classic distal RTA characteristically causes hypokalaemia, not hyperkalaemia (Harrison); the stored explanation itself concedes this.

### `neetpg-2013-s1-q1641` — Microbiology (AIPGMEE 2013)

*History of yellow green watery discharge and pruritus?*

- **Was:** B. Candida
- **Now:** A. Trichomonas vaginalis

Yellow-green frothy discharge with pruritus and a raised vaginal pH is Trichomonas, while candidiasis gives a thick white curdy discharge at normal pH; the stored explanation is unrelated boilerplate about Rh disease, so the key has no support at all.

### `neetpg-2014-s1-q1266` — Medicine (AIPGMEE 2014)

*1 year old male child is having Heart Rate 40/min, BP 90/60. His serum Potassium = 6.5 what is the next best management?*

- **Was:** C. Sodium bicarbonate
- **Now:** D. Calcium chloride

With ECG or haemodynamic manifestations of hyperkalaemia, every current protocol including PALS and Harrison gives intravenous calcium first to stabilise the myocardial membrane, with bicarbonate only a slow and unreliable shifting agent; here the bradycardia makes the cardiotoxicity explicit, and calcium chloride is the only calcium option offered.

### `neetpg-2015-s1-q0341` — Biochemistry (AIPGMEE 2015)

*Vitamin formed in the body -*

- **Was:** A. B 1
- **Now:** B. B 3

Niacin is synthesised endogenously from tryptophan (60:1) via the kynurenine pathway, the standard textbook example of a vitamin formed in the body; thiamine is only produced by colonic flora, which is not human synthesis. The stored explanation itself concludes B3.

### `neetpg-2016-s1-q0473` — Pathology (AIPGMEE 2016)

*Vitamin deficiency associated with cystic fibrosis is*

- **Was:** D. B12
- **Now:** A. K

Pancreatic insufficiency in cystic fibrosis causes fat malabsorption and deficiency of vitamins A, D, E and K (Robbins, Nelson); only vitamin K appears among the options, and the stored explanation itself admits K is the defensible answer.

### `neetpg-2016-s1-q1750` — Microbiology (AIPGMEE 2016)

*A Bone marrow transplant recipient patientdeveloped chest infection. On HRCT 'Tree in bud appearance' is seen. Most likely causative agent is:*

- **Was:** B. Pneumocystis
- **Now:** C. TB

Tree-in-bud reflects endobronchial spread and is classically tuberculosis (Harrison, standard HRCT teaching); Pneumocystis produces ground-glass opacity and is not a tree-in-bud pathogen, so the keyed option is not defensible in any host.

### `neetpg-2021-s1-q0075` — Community Medicine (NEET PG 2021)

*A pregnant woman whose niece contracted varicella in same house was negative for serum antibodies of varicella. What would this mean?*

- **Was:** A. She is susceptible to zoster
- **Now:** B. She is susceptible to chicken pox

Zoster is reactivation and requires prior primary infection, so a seronegative person cannot be 'susceptible to zoster'; a VZV IgG-negative exposed pregnant woman is susceptible to chickenpox and needs VZIG within 96 hours.

### `neetpg-2021-s1-q0139` — Obstetrics & Gynaecology (NEET PG 2021)

*H/o recurrent abortions at 8,11& 22 weeks. Cardiac activity is normal in all the three. H/o of preeclampsia in last pregnancy. What is the most probable cause*

- **Was:** A. Syphilis
- **Now:** B. APLA

Fetal death at or beyond 10 weeks with documented cardiac activity plus pre-eclampsia are two of the Sydney obstetric criteria for antiphospholipid syndrome (Dutta, Williams); syphilis explains neither the 8-week loss with a live heart nor the pre-eclampsia.
